## Other helpful documentations

ProteomIQon uses different open-source libraries. For developers, we recommend exploring them as well.

1. [MzIO](https://csbiology.github.io/MzIO/index.html)
2. [BioFSharp](https://www.biofsharp.com/BioFSharp/)
3. [FSharp.Stats](https://fslab.org/FSharp.Stats/)

## Available on Galaxy and WorkflowHub

1. **Galaxy**

   You can find all ProteomIQon tools on [Galaxy](https://usegalaxy.eu/); there you will also find a beginner tutorial.

2. **WorkflowHub**

   You can also find an overview of ProteomIQon on [WorkflowHub](https://workflowhub.org/workflows/2051).
