AlignmentBasedQuantStatistics

A peptide ion transferred by QuantBasedAlignment and quantified by AlignmentBasedQuantification has no identification in the run, so nothing yet says whether the quantified peak is the ion. AlignmentBasedQuantStatistics gives every such ion two values:

  • AlignmentScore: the probability that the quantified peak is the ion's peak, given that the analyte is present in the run.
  • AlignmentQValue: the estimated share of false quantifications among the new ions of the run that score at least as well. A quantification is false when the chosen peak is not the ion's peak or, unless AbsentAnalyteFdr is Off, when the analyte is absent from the run.

New ions are the transferred ions without a PSM based quantification in the run.

JoinQuantPepIonsWithProteins reads the output. LabelFreeProteinQuantification and LabeledProteinQuantification filter on the q-value only if their Alignment_QValue parameter is set. They then drop transferred rows with a q-value at or above it. All their default files set it to Some 0.05, and None keeps every transferred row.

Training data and label

The tool learns from overlap ions: ions that a run identified and quantified itself and that were also transferred to it. For them the run's own PSM based peak is the reference. Let z be the distance between the transferred peak and the own peak, in units of the own peak's standard deviation.

Label

Condition

Use

right

z ≤ PositiveRtSdFactor (1) and |log2 (transferred / own quantity)| ≤ MaxAbsLog2QuantRatio (1)

training

ambiguous

PositiveRtSdFactor < z ≤ NegativeRtSdFactor (3), same quantity bound

left out of training

wrong

everything else

training

Overlap ions whose own peak cannot serve as reference (no fitted peak, or a width or quantity that is not positive) get no label.

Features

All features come from the transferred row of AlignmentBasedQuantification and the .align row. None uses the ion's own PSM row, so the features of an overlap ion look like those of a new ion.

Group

Features

scan time

residual to PredictedScanTime (signed, absolute, per peak sd, per local alignment error), the local alignment error, peak width and its ratio to the source peak

mass and isotopes

|ppm| of the monoisotopic peak, intensity weighted |ppm| over the isotope peaks, number of isotope peaks, similarity of the isotope pattern to the source, KL divergences to the theoretical pattern

chromatogram

correlation with the source trace (around the peak and over the whole XIC), DTW distance to the source trace, competing peaks in the XIC, share of the XIC maximum at the apex, XIC length, relative SEO

ion

source quantity and apex intensity, source scan time, m/z, charge

selection

from <run>.alignfeatures: Sel_NCandidates, Sel_IsoCorr, Sel_IsoRatioLog2, Sel_ShapeCorr, Sel_RtZ, Sel_ImZ, Sel_LogRelHeight, Sel_AbsLog2FwhmRatio, and Sel_CandidateProbability, Sel_CandidateMargin when all runs used the same selection model

ion mobility

|IonMobility − PredictedIonMobility| / PredictedIonMobilitySd (timsTOF only)

The local alignment error of an ion is the median |own PSM scan time − PredictedScanTime| of the run's overlap ions predicted within ±2 min. Every run needs <run>.alignfeatures next to its aligned .quant.

UseIntensityRatioFeatures adds four features: the ratios of target to source quantity and apex intensity, and the target quantity and apex intensity. They are off by default: a model that prefers a ratio near 1 penalises peptides that really changed between the samples.

Model, calibration and q-values

  1. The overlap ions of all learning runs are pooled and split into 5 folds by peptide sequence, so the forms of a peptide stay together.
  2. Per fold, a gradient boosted tree model (ML.NET FastTree) is trained on the right and wrong ions of the other four folds. Unless AbsentAnalyteFdr is Off, the decoys of the learning runs join this training as wrong examples, in the fold of their peptide.
  3. Per fold, an isotonic calibration (the share of right ions as a non-decreasing function of the raw score) is fitted on the right and wrong overlap ions of the held-out fold. It turns scores into probabilities. Below the lowest fitted score the probability follows a logistic fit of the label on the raw score. A fold with fewer than 200 such ions, or with one label only, is calibrated on the held-out ions of all folds. If those are too few or of one label as well, the logistic fit alone is used.
  4. The AlignmentScore p of a new ion is the mean of the calibrated probabilities of the 5 fold models.
  5. The posterior error probability of a new ion is 1 − p. Per run, the new ions are sorted by p in descending order. The AlignmentQValue of an ion is the mean posterior error probability of all new ions with at least its p, made monotone.
  6. Unless AbsentAnalyteFdr is Off, the absent-analyte rate at the ion's score (see Absent analytes) is added to the q-value, which is made monotone again.

The highest probability the isotonic calibration can give grows with the number of right and wrong overlap ions it is fitted on: about 0.99 up to 2,000 ions, 0.999 from 20,000. With few overlap ions a cutoff of q ≤ 0.01 can therefore keep few or no transferred ions.

The log reports an out-of-fold check. Each overlap ion is scored by the fold model that did not see it and calibrated on the held-out ions of the other folds (by the logistic fit alone if those are too few or of one label). The true share of wrong peaks is given at q ≤ 0.01, 0.05 and 0.1, once with the ambiguous ions counted as wrong and once without them.

Absent analytes

The overlap ions are present in the run, so they cannot show how the model scores an ion whose analyte is absent. The decoy transfers of AlignmentBasedQuantification (<run>.aligndecoy) are used for that. Per run, the absent-analyte rate at score t is

FDR_absent(t) = π · (decoys with p ≥ t / all decoys) · N_new / N_new(p ≥ t)

with N_new the number of new ions of the run and π the fraction of new ions whose analyte is absent. Estimated estimates π per run as Storey does, with λ = 0.5. Conservative sets π to 1.

  • A run with fewer than 200 decoys uses the decoys of all runs to score.
  • Decoys without a fitted peak and new ions without an alignment row count as p = 0.
  • When a run to score or to learn from has no <run>.aligndecoy next to its aligned .quant (AlignmentBasedQuantification writes none with DecoyFraction 0), or no decoy has a fitted peak, the tool warns and works as Off.

Each flag takes one file, several files, or a directory. The runs to learn from are usually the runs to score. Without -mf the files are paired by position, the files of a directory sorted by name, and the numbers of files must agree.

For every scored run the tool writes <run>.quant with the layout of the input .quant files: the PSM based rows of -i and one row per new ion with AlignmentScore and AlignmentQValue. When the PSM row of an overlap ion has no fitted peak, the transferred row replaces it and is scored like a new ion. -ts writes <run>.quanttestset per learning run, with the overlap ions, their label, fold, raw and calibrated out-of-fold score, out-of-fold q-value and features. Logs go to AlignmentBasedQuantStatistics_log.txt, with the features, the out-of-fold check and the calibration per score bin and per source intensity quartile, and to one <run>_log.txt per run.

Writing a parameter file

open ProteomIQon

let alignmentBasedQuantStatisticsParams : Dto.AlignmentBasedQuantStatisticsParams =
    {
        PositiveRtSdFactor         = 1.
        NegativeRtSdFactor         = 3.
        MaxAbsLog2QuantRatio       = 1.
        UseIntensityRatioFeatures  = false
        UseIonMobility             = true
        NumberOfFolds              = 5
        Trainer                    = "FastTree"
        // empty: the defaults of the trainer
        NumberOfLeaves             = System.Nullable()
        NumberOfTrees              = System.Nullable()
        MinimumExampleCountPerLeaf = System.Nullable()
        LearningRate               = System.Nullable()
        AbsentAnalyteFdr           = "Estimated"
        Seed                       = 1
    }

// Replace the temp folder with your project folder.
let outputPath =
    System.IO.Path.Combine(System.IO.Path.GetTempPath(), "AlignmentBasedQuantStatisticsParams.json")

Json.serializeAndWrite outputPath alignmentBasedQuantStatisticsParams

Running the tool

Install the tool with dotnet tool install --global ProteomIQon.AlignmentBasedQuantStatistics. Score every run, learn from all of them, pair the files by name, and work on four runs at a time:

proteomiqon-alignmentbasedquantstatistics -i path/to/quant -ii path/to/align -iii path/to/aligned -l path/to/quant -ll path/to/align -lll path/to/aligned -o path/to/output -p path/to/AlignmentBasedQuantStatisticsParams.json -mf -c 4

Add -ts to keep the training ions and -dc for the score histogram. Print the description of every argument:

proteomiqon-alignmentbasedquantstatistics --help

Limitations

  • The false discovery rate of absent analytes is an estimate from decoys at a shifted scan time. Analytes near the detection limit are not modelled.
  • The model needs overlap ions. With few identifications per run, or runs that share few ions, there is little to learn from.
